ProofPay

A freelancer milestone helper: an AI reads the submitted evidence text and gives a recommendation. You decide what to invoice.

1. FreelancerCreates a milestone and submits proof text.
2. AI checkReads the proof text only. Never opens files or checks quality. A recommendation, not a decision.
3. You approveApproving only sends a PayPal invoice to the client. It does not move money.
4. Client paysThe client pays the invoice separately in PayPal. Refresh status reads it back from PayPal.
